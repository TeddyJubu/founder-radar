# Fund criteria review — 30 September 2026

The four funds still score companies from the Google Sheet's live rules. Hard eligibility and the editable 0–4 preference matrix are separate: a preference score describes this model's judgement, not a promise that a fund will invest.

## Published mandates checked

- **DSW:** its SEIS programme principally targets regional technology and spinouts outside the London–Oxford–Cambridge triangle. Its EIS service remains represented as a regional preference. London stays eligible for EIS, but Today suggests another genuinely viable fund when one exists and explains that choice. [DSW SEIS](https://dsw.vc/dsw-ventures-seis-fund-2/), [DSW investor services](https://dsw.vc/for-investors/).
- **Northstar:** EIS Growth primarily targets northern England, late seed to Series A, with commercial traction. It remains a soft northern preference; the specialist regional vehicles retain their hard place rules. [Northstar EIS Growth](https://www.northstarventures.co.uk/funds/eis-fund/).
- **Outward:** the current website includes finance, insurance, cybersecurity, property, legal services, healthcare, professional services and work/HR. The old description incorrectly required a finance layer for every company. The description and preferred sectors now represent the broader regulated-industry thesis. Legal technology, property technology, cybersecurity, work/HR technology and data infrastructure have distinct editable model categories. Existing client-authored matrix cells are preserved; only new category defaults are added. Generic software does not acquire a strong regulated-industry match merely from its registration code. [Outward's published thesis](https://outwardvc.com/).
- **Anticus:** Finance Yorkshire's Seedcorn and Growth streams support businesses across Yorkshire and Humber. The region remains the binding filter; no artificial companies are added to meet a fund quota. [Finance Yorkshire appointment and scope](https://www.finance-yorkshire.com/news-and-events/finance-yorkshire-launches-new-fund-to-support-smes).

Published fund pages do not confirm every numerical threshold in the client's model. Existing age, funding and coverage thresholds remain visible and editable as model settings. Unpublished cheque floors are still identified as unverified; investor minimum subscriptions are not treated as company cheque sizes.

## Selection is separate from scoring

Match and Fresh continue to use the same deterministic calculation. Today softly changes ordering to spread available companies across source families, funds and sectors. It never promotes a rejected fund, invents a company, changes a score to create balance, or discards the rest of the backlog. A thin genuinely eligible pool can still be uneven; the interface must show its actual composition honestly.

The company and its exact recommendation must complete the evidence-link and Hermes checks before it is shown. Missing or unfinished checks are a visible waiting queue, not approval.
