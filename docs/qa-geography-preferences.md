# Final review follows the configured geography rule

The morning checker previously rejected London, Oxford and Cambridge against a regional fund regardless of whether that vehicle used HARD or SOFT geography. The live review confirmed geography-only rejections against Northstar EIS Growth even though its configured regional rule is SOFT. This could remove an otherwise eligible company and distort the fund mix.

A HARD rule remains an eligibility restriction. A SOFT regional rule remains a preference used by deterministic scoring; being outside that preferred region alone must not veto the whole company. The final checker still checks identity, startup status, existing backing and other eligibility conditions. No fund rules, scores, thresholds or quotas change.

Only cards with a SOFT regional focus gain the explicit policy note in their evidence payload and reviewer instructions. Their snapshot changes, so their old approval or rejection cannot substitute for a genuine new check. Other cards keep exactly their existing payload and instructions, allowing their genuine current checks to remain reusable. Unknown rule modes retain the existing conservative precheck; configuration validation handles malformed fund rules.

This does not hard-block every London DSW vehicle or impose per-fund quotas. The existing London recommendation warning/alternative policy remains unchanged.
