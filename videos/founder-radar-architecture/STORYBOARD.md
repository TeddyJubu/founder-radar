## Video direction

One drafting-table world for the whole piece. The notebook is the persistent motif — frames 4 through 10 should feel like turning or drawing on the same page, not cutting to a new app. Type is large enough to read at 1920×1080 without leaning in. Reveals follow the voice: each named object appears when it is spoken, never all at t=0. Stage 6 is the only impact stamp; everything else is hairline, pan, and assemble. Captions on. No website capture. No stock founders.
